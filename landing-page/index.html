04 / BEFORE YOU DIVE IN

Good questions.

Here are a few to get you started.

Do I need to know how to code?+

No prior technical skills are needed. The workshop starts with the basics of guiding AI and checking its output.

Who is the event for?+

The event’s eligibility section lists ages 15–25: students in Singapore and young working adults. See Luma for the organiser’s full eligibility details.

Can I come without a team?+

Yes. Join individually or with friends; teams of 3–4 form around shared interests at the hackathon.

How do I get ready to build?+

You can open both demos right in your browser. To edit the code, follow the laptop setup guide. The examples need no API key or paid subscription.

Can I still register?+

Registration is closed on Luma. Visit the event page to contact the host or follow updates. The workshop resources remain available here.
