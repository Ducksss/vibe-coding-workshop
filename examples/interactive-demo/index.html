01 / THE IDEA

Building can be lonely.
Dinner shouldn’t be.

The best conversations rarely happen on a stage. They happen when the phones go away, the food arrives, and someone says, “Honestly? Me too.”

We’re bringing founders together around one table. No panels. No performance. Just space to connect as people.

05 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few things,
before you join.

Do I need to have raised funding?+

No. This table is for founders at every stage, including those still working on their first idea.

Is this a pitching or networking event?+

The focus is a shared meal and thoughtful conversation. You don’t need a deck, an elevator pitch, or a polished success story.

What happens after I register?+

Your interest is recorded. Once the host confirms the date, venue, availability, and any cost, you can decide whether to attend. This form is not a booking or payment.
